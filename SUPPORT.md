# Support

**Questions, confusion and "am I holding this wrong"** → open a
[discussion or an issue](../../issues). Answers belong somewhere searchable,
which is why there is no chat channel: a question answered in a Discord is a
question the next person asks again.

**A defect** → an issue, with what you ran and what happened.

**A security vulnerability** → *not* an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**A disagreement about a refusal** — your implementation rejects something
ours accepts, or the reverse → an issue, and thank you. That is the report
this project is most glad to receive.

## What we can promise

Nothing on a clock. This is maintained by a small team alongside other work,
and pretending otherwise would be the sort of commitment nobody is held to.
What we will do is answer, in public, including when the answer is that we are
not going to do something.
