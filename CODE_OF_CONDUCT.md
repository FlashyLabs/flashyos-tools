# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be direct about work and generous about people. Criticise a design, a
measurement or a line of code as hard as you like; do not criticise the person
who wrote it.

Specifically, in this project:

- **Disagreement is the point.** A maintainer is wrong sometimes and the issue
  tracker is where that gets found out.
- **"It works on my machine" is a measurement**, and saying which machine is
  the difference between a report and an opinion.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and sustained disruption are not welcome, and
  neither is the style of argument whose purpose is to exhaust somebody.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through this repository's Security tab (**Report a
vulnerability** opens a private channel) or to the maintainers directly.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, and block accounts. A
maintainer who acts under this document says that they did and why, in public,
unless doing so would identify a reporter who asked not to be identified.

## Scope

This repository, its issues, its pull requests, and any space where somebody is
representing the project.
